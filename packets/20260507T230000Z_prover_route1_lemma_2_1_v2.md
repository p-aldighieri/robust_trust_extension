## The setting (carried from formalizer + breakdown v2)

$\Omega$ finite, $\tau$ Borel probability on $\Delta := \Delta(\Omega)$, $M = \mathrm{supp}\,\tau$, $W \subset \mathbb{R}^\Omega$ compact convex, $\alpha \in (0, 1)$. $F(C) := \int_M [\alpha \max_{w\in C}\,s\cdot w + (1-\alpha)\min_{w\in C}\,s\cdot w]\,\tau(ds)$. Optimal labeled menu $(C^*, w^*)$ with $C^* = \overline{w^*(M)}$. $D_E := \overline{w^*(M\setminus E)}$, $\text{Del}(w^*, \tau) := \{D_E : E\in\mathcal B(M),\,\tau(E)>0,\,D_E\subsetneq C^*\}$. (H_del): $\forall D \in \text{Del}, F(D) < F(C^*)$.

## The repaired LP (from Lemma 1.1')

For finite Borel partition $\mathcal P = \{A_1, \ldots, A_n\}$ of $M$, finite test set $V_0 \subset W$, ε > 0:

- **Distinct cell labels** $U_{\mathcal P} = \{u_1, \ldots, u_r\}$ where $u_j$ are the distinct values of $\{w^*(m_j)\}$ (duplicate labels quotiented).
- **Label fibers** $L_u := \{s \in M : w^*(s) = u\}$.
- **Row-minimizer flags** $R_u^\eps := \{s \in M : s\cdot u \le \min_{z\in U_{\mathcal P}}\,s\cdot z + \eps\}$.
- **Slabs** $\mathcal S$: the finite Borel partition of $M$ generated by $\mathcal P \cup \{L_u : u \in U_{\mathcal P}\} \cup \{R_u^\eps : u \in U_{\mathcal P}\}$.
- **Variables:** $x_{B, u} \ge 0$ for $B \in \mathcal S$, $u \in U_{\mathcal P}$ — unscaled misaligned source mass from slab $B$ routed to column $u$.

**Constraints:**
- Row mass: $\sum_{u \in J(B)} x_{B, u} = t_B := \tau(B)$, where $J(B) := \{u : B \subseteq R_u^\eps\}$. $x_{B, u} = 0$ for $u \notin J(B)$.
- **Sourcewise aligned attribution:** the aligned-truthful component to column $u$ is exactly $\alpha\,\tau(L_u)$.
- **Primitive cone test:** for every $u \in U_{\mathcal P}$, $v \in V_0$,
$$\alpha\sum_B \int_{B \cap L_u} s\cdot(v - u)\,d\tau(s) + (1-\alpha)\sum_B x_{B, u}\,\bar s_B \cdot (v - u) \le 0,$$
where $\bar s_B := t_B^{-1}\!\int_B s\,d\tau$.

## Lemma 2.1' (candidate statement)

**Suppose $\mathrm{LP}'(\mathcal P, V_0, \eps)$ is infeasible** for some finite Borel partition $\mathcal P$, finite $V_0 \subset W$, and $\eps > 0$.

**Then** there exists a Borel $E \subseteq M$ with $\tau(E) > 0$, $D_E := \overline{w^*(M\setminus E)} \subsetneq C^*$ proper compact, and $F(D_E) \ge F(C^*) - O(\eps)$, contradicting $(H_\text{del})$ in the limit $\eps \downarrow 0$.

The dual certificate must:
1. Translate Farkas multipliers into a **source patch $E = w^{*-1}(O)$** for some relatively open label patch $O \subset C^*$.
2. Verify $\tau(E) > 0$ from the certificate.
3. Verify $D_E \subsetneq C^*$ properly (the **Borel→compact** step in this v2 setting).
4. Compare $F(D_E)$ to $F(C^*)$ using only primitive inequalities, with at most $O(\eps)$ slack.

The breakdown's renaming-test note: "Passes only if $E$ is described as a source patch cut out by $w^*$, primitive inequalities, and $\tau$. Fails if the certificate says 'delete the messages whose induced posterior leaves a belief set.'"

## The new finite-mass-fiber problem

The breakdown v2 surfaced one new structural issue: in the continuum case, **exact label fibers $L_u$ may have zero $\tau$-mass** (if $w^*$ is not constant on positive-mass sets for each label). The aligned mass $\alpha\,\tau(L_u)$ would then be zero for every $u$, and the LP loses its sourcewise aligned-attribution power.

Address this in your proof or counterexample. Two natural responses:
- **Discretization-only case:** prove Lemma 2.1' for discrete $w^*$ (finitely many distinct labels with positive $\tau$-mass each). The continuum case becomes a limit issue handled by the limit lemmas.
- **Label-neighborhood regularization:** replace exact fibers $L_u$ with neighborhoods $L_u^\delta := \{s : \|w^*(s) - u\| < \delta\}$ for $\delta > 0$. The LP construction extends naturally if labels are picked from a δ-net. Show the LP is feasible for every (sufficiently fine) discretization under (H_del).
