\begin{abstract}
This note is the v9 layer above the v8 menu-engine exposition. It records
the third pass at the infinite extension of Theorem~2 of Dworczak \&
Smolin's \emph{Robust Trust} (2026), motivated by Piotr Dworczak's
2026-05-20 suggestion to reformulate the agent's strategy as a compact
subset of the weak Pareto frontier $W^P$ of state-contingent payoff
profiles, with the misaligned adviser mapping signals directly into
payoff vectors in the chosen subset. We report four new results.
\textbf{(T1)~Finite-menu Pareto-Hall calibration} closes
\emph{unconditionally} in payoff-label coordinates: at any
finite-menu Pareto-completed ambient local maximizer of the value
functional, Clarke--Danskin stationarity produces measurable active-face
weights $\lambda^\pm:M\to\Delta(k)$ whose normalized images
$p_i = g_i/q_i$ lie in the Bayes-cone $B_W(w_i)$. Calibration
\emph{emerges} as a Lagrange multiplier rather than being imposed as an
external Hall-type assumption. \textbf{(T2)~$\alpha=0$ unconditional}
infinite-$M$ Theorem~2 closes via a singleton-strategy reduction:
when the adviser is purely adversarial, the optimal payoff-profile
menu collapses to a single Bayes-optimal-at-prior profile, and the
adviser's fixed-message strategy makes the agent's continuation
Bayes-optimal at the prior. \textbf{(T3)~Compact-menu Pareto-Hall}
closes in payoff-label coordinates under finite effective exposure
of the optimal menu, a primitive structural condition strictly
weaker than $v8$ menu-Hall in those coordinates. \textbf{(T4)~Honest
endpoint.} For general $\alpha\in(0,1)$ and the original-game
Definition~2, the lift from payoff-label calibration to messagewise
calibration requires a finite-fiber matching condition \emph{(D2)}
which is shown to be the finite-fiber version of $v8$'s menu-Hall,
not a strictly weaker primitive. The deletion-compatible Hall duality
theorem named in the project closure memo remains the single open
object. A fresh seven-candidate primitive search (atomless plus
fiber-richness, single-valued Gauss map, $\tau$-symmetry, smooth plus
strictly convex $W^P$, product utility, coarsening, fiber-rich
Lagrangian transport) did not produce a strictly weaker primitive.
All claims above were verified by independent reviewer passes on fresh
chats; the pipeline trace is in \S\ref{sec:pipeline}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setting and recap of $v8$}\label{sec:setting}

We use the notation of \emph{Robust Trust} and of $v8$ (\S\ref{sec:setting}
in \texttt{exposition.pdf}). $\Om$ is finite with full-support prior
$\mu_0$; $s\in\Delta(\Om)$ has unconditional law $\tau$ on
$M = \suppset(\tau)$; $\theta\in\Th$ (compact metric) is drawn from
$f(\cdot\mid\omega)$, independent of $s$ given $\omega$; $A$ is compact
metric; $u(a,\omega,\theta)$ is bounded continuous in $a$. With
probability $\alpha\in[0,1]$ the adviser is aligned and reports $s$
truthfully; with probability $1-\alpha$ misaligned, using any
$\beta:M\to\Delta(M)$ Borel. Agent strategies are
$\sigma:M\times\Th\to\Delta(A)$ Borel. The robust value is
$U^* = \sup_{\sigma\in\Si}U(\sigma)$ with $U$ as in $v8$ Eq.~(1).

The $v8$ menu engine works on the payoff-profile polytope
$W = \{w\in\R^N : \exists\,\hat\sigma:\Th\to\Delta(A),\;w(\omega)=\E_{\hat\sigma}[u(a,\omega,\theta)\mid\omega]\}$,
which is convex compact (\emph{Robust Trust} Lemma~2 of Theorem~1's
proof, p.~27). Its weak Pareto frontier $W^P$ is the closed subset of
profiles not strictly state-wise dominated. Lemma~2 records the key
equivalence \emph{$\hat\sigma$ is Bayes-optimal for some belief iff its
profile is in $W^P$}.

$v8$ delivers a three-tier result:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Tier~1a (value optimality)}: $\sup_C F(C)$ is attained on
    $\K(W)$ under standing alone, where
    $F(C) = \int[\alpha\max_C s\!\cdot w + (1-\alpha)\min_C s\!\cdot w]\,d\tau$.
  \item \textbf{Tier~1b (exact adversary)}: under \emph{exact-contact},
    a deterministic message kernel $\beta^*(\cdot\mid s) = \delta_{m^*(s)}$
    attains the misaligned infimum.
  \item \textbf{Tier~2 (full robust rationalizability)}: under
    \emph{exact-contact} plus \emph{menu-Hall}, Definition~2 holds
    $q$-a.e.
\end{itemize}
The $v8$ closure memo names the single open object as a
\emph{deletion-compatible Hall duality theorem} reconciling sourcewise
deletion certificates with messagewise Bayes-cone calibration; both
prior attack routes (constrained-persuasion duality and
calibration-defect bound) stalled at the same structural family of
gaps.

\section{The Pareto-frontier reformulation}\label{sec:gp}

\paragraph{Piotr's proposal (2026-05-20, verbatim).} ``Instead of
thinking of the Agent as selecting a mapping from private signals and
messages of the Adviser to distributions over actions, we can think of
the Agent as selecting a subset of the weak Pareto frontier of
state-contingent payoffs, relying on Lemma~2 in the proof of
Theorem~1. With some appropriate topology, the set of all feasible
subsets is compact. The strategy of the misaligned Adviser is to map
their signal into a state-contingent payoff vector in the set chosen
by the Agent.''

\paragraph{The game $\mathcal G_P$.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Agent's strategy space: $\K(W^P) = \{C\subseteq W^P : C\ne\emptyset,\,C\text{ compact}\}$,
    equipped with Hausdorff distance. Blaschke selection yields
    $(\K(W^P), d_H)$ compact metric.
  \item Misaligned adviser's strategy space for fixed $C$: Borel maps
    $\beta:M\to W^P$ with $\beta(s)\in C$ $\tau$-a.e. Compactness follows
    from the relaxed-Young embedding into $\Delta(M\times C)$.
  \item Payoff $U_P(C,\beta) = \alpha\!\int_M\!\max_{w\in C}s\!\cdot w\,d\tau + (1-\alpha)\!\int_M s\!\cdot\beta(s)\,d\tau$.
  \item Agent's robust value $V_P(C) = \inf_\beta U_P(C,\beta) = \alpha\!\int\max_C s\!\cdot w\,d\tau + (1-\alpha)\!\int\min_C s\!\cdot w\,d\tau$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{WLOG equivalence to the original value.}
By Lemma~2 of Theorem~1's proof, every optimal $\sigma\in\Si$ is
equivalent to one whose message-conditional profile $w_\sigma(m)$ takes
values in $W^P$ for every $m\in M$. Setting $C_\sigma := \overline{w_\sigma(M)}\subseteq W^P$
collapses the adversary's effective action set to $C_\sigma$ and yields
\begin{equation}\label{eq:wlog}
  U^* \;=\; \sup_{C\in\K(W^P)}V_P(C).
\end{equation}
The lift from any $C\in\K(W^P)$ to a measurable original-game strategy
is the profile-realization right inverse $R:W\to\hat\Si$ (KRN; $v8$
\S3 sub-lemma).

\paragraph{What $\mathcal G_P$ buys cleanly.} The adversary's
best-response in $\mathcal G_P$ is $\beta^*(s)\in\argmin_{w\in C}s\!\cdot w$,
existence automatic by Kuratowski--Ryll-Nardzewski on the compact-valued
correspondence $s\mapsto C$. This dispenses with the $v8/v5$ \emph{A8c-lsc}
hypothesis. Tier~1a in payoff-vector form is therefore strictly cleaner
than $v8$'s.

\section{Finite-menu Pareto-Hall calibration via Clarke--Danskin}\label{sec:t1}

The key technical novelty of $v9$ is a Lagrange-multiplier-based
calibration theorem that does not assume any Hall-type kernel
existence.

\begin{theorem}[Finite-menu Pareto-Hall calibration]\label{thm:T1}
Let $C^* = \{w_1,\ldots,w_k\}\subseteq W^P$ be a Pareto-completed ambient
local maximizer of the finite-menu functional
\[
  F_k(w_1,\ldots,w_k) \;=\; \int_M\!\big[\alpha\max_i s\!\cdot w_i + (1-\alpha)\min_i s\!\cdot w_i\big]\,\tau(ds).
\]
Then there exist Borel measurable active-face weights
\[
  \lambda^+:M\to\Delta(k),\quad \lambda^-:M\to\Delta(k),
\]
with $\suppset\lambda^+(s)\subseteq\argmax_i s\!\cdot w_i$ and
$\suppset\lambda^-(s)\subseteq\argmin_i s\!\cdot w_i$ for $\tau$-a.e.\ $s$,
such that the integral subgradient component
\[
  g_i \;=\; \alpha\!\int\lambda^+_i(s)\,s\,d\tau + (1-\alpha)\!\int\lambda^-_i(s)\,s\,d\tau
\]
satisfies $g_i\in N_W(w_i)$ for every $i$; and with
$q_i = \alpha\!\int\lambda^+_i\,d\tau + (1-\alpha)\!\int\lambda^-_i\,d\tau$,
the normalized vector $p_i := g_i/q_i$ lies in $B_W(w_i) = N_W(w_i)\cap\Delta(\Om)$
for every $i$ with $q_i > 0$.
\end{theorem}

\noindent The proof chain is twelve lemmas long; here we record the
load-bearing structure.

\begin{lemma}[Integral Clarke--Danskin representation]\label{lem:L6}
Every $g\in\partial_C F_k(\bar w)$ admits Borel multipliers
$\lambda^\pm:M\to\Delta(k)$ with $\suppset\lambda^\pm(s)$ in the active
max/min faces and the integral representation above.
\end{lemma}

\noindent Proof outline. Apply Clarke's integral subdifferential
interchange (Clarke 1983, \S2.7) to obtain
$\partial_C F_k(\bar w)\subseteq\int_M\partial_C\phi_s(\bar w)\,d\tau$
(Aumann integral closed in finite dimension; uniform Lipschitz
$L(s)=\|s\|_\infty\le 1$). Use the larger active-weight correspondence
$R(s)\supseteq\partial_C\phi_s(\bar w)$ rather than equality
(\emph{patched after reviewer pass~01}: equality fails on positive-measure
tie sets in general). Select a Borel measurable selector
$\xi(s)\in R(s)$ on the Borel partition $\{E_{I,J}\}$ of $M$ into
active-cell strata, apply Kuratowski--Ryll-Nardzewski to decompose
$\xi(s)$ into $\lambda^+(s)\in\Delta(I)$ and $\lambda^-(s)\in\Delta(J)$.

\begin{lemma}[Clarke--Fermat normal-cone stationarity]\label{lem:L7}
If $\bar w$ is an ambient local maximizer of $F_k$ on $W^k$, then there
exists $g\in\partial_C F_k(\bar w)$ with $g\in N_{W^k}(\bar w) = \prod_i N_W(w_i)$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent Proof outline. Apply Clarke's necessary condition for the
constrained minimum of $-F_k$ on $W^k$, yielding
$0\in\partial_C(-F_k)(\bar w) + N_{W^k}(\bar w)$, and use the product
normal-cone formula. The ambient (rather than frontier-only) local-max
hypothesis is essential for $N_W$-normality and is delivered by Lemma~2
of Theorem~1's proof under standard Pareto-completion.

\begin{lemma}[Multipliers are the calibration kernel]\label{lem:L8}
With $g$ from Lemma~\ref{lem:L7} and $\lambda^\pm$ from Lemma~\ref{lem:L6},
the mass balance $\sum_i q_i = 1$ holds, $p_i := g_i/q_i\in\Delta(\Om)$
for $q_i>0$, and $p_i\in B_W(w_i)$.
\end{lemma}

\noindent Proof outline. \emph{Mass balance}: integrate
$\sum_i\lambda^\pm_i = 1$ against $\tau$. \emph{Posterior in simplex}:
$\sum_\omega[g_i]_\omega = \alpha\!\int\lambda^+_i\sum_\omega s(\omega)\,d\tau + (1-\alpha)\!\int\lambda^-_i\sum_\omega s(\omega)\,d\tau = q_i$
since $s\in\Delta(\Om)$; nonneg entries by $s\ge 0$ and $\lambda^\pm\ge 0$.
\emph{Calibration}: $g_i\in N_W(w_i)$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:L7};
$N_W(w_i)$ is a convex cone; positive rescaling $p_i = g_i/q_i$
preserves cone membership; intersection with $\Delta(\Om)$ gives $B_W(w_i)$.

\noindent\textbf{Mechanism.} The Clarke multipliers $\lambda^\pm$ are
not bookkeeping: they encode \emph{both} the aligned tie-routing weights
on the supporting hyperplane $\argmax_i s\!\cdot w_i$ \emph{and} the
adversarial tie-routing weights on $\argmin_i s\!\cdot w_i$. The
normalization $p_i = g_i/q_i$ is the disintegration posterior at
label~$i$ under a (label-coordinate) joint law combining aligned
$\lambda^+$-routing with weight~$\alpha$ and adversarial
$\lambda^-$-routing with weight~$1-\alpha$. Fermat normality of $g_i$
in $N_W(w_i)$ is therefore \emph{equivalent} to Bayes-optimality of the
disintegrated posterior at $w_i$: calibration emerges from the
finite-menu optimization itself, not from an external Hall assumption.

\paragraph{No hidden hypotheses.} The proof uses no atomlessness of
$\tau$, no genericity (positive-measure ties are handled by measurable
selection on Borel active-cell strata), no strict convexity of $W$, and
no $\tau$-AC restriction. Reviewer~02 (fresh chat) verified
Lemmas~\ref{lem:L6}--\ref{lem:L8} with all hypotheses on the table.

\paragraph{$v8$ sharpness package compatibility.} Theorem~\ref{thm:T1}
does not contradict $v8$ Lemma~7 (cone intersection) or Theorem~8
(no-free-dust) on the WTA ternary witness, because the witness is
\emph{not} a finite ambient local maximizer of $F_k$ in the
Lemma~\ref{lem:L7} sense. The $v8$ closure memo classifies the witness
as a menu-engine artefact; this classification is what allows
Theorem~\ref{thm:T1} to hold without contradicting the witness's
non-calibration.

\section{$\alpha=0$ unconditional via singleton strategy}\label{sec:t2}

When the adviser is purely adversarial, the optimal payoff-profile menu
collapses to a single Bayes-optimal-at-prior profile, and Definition~2
closes by a brute-force calculation.

\begin{theorem}[$\alpha=0$ unconditional Theorem~2]\label{thm:T2}
Under the standing hypotheses and $\alpha=0$ (pure adversarial adviser),
there exists a robustly rationalizable optimal strategy without any
finiteness of $M$ or $\Th$. Explicitly:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Pick $w_0\in\argmax_{w\in W}\mu_0\!\cdot w$, attained because $W$
    is compact in $\R^N$.
  \item Set $\hat\sigma^*(m) := R(w_0)$ for every $m\in M$ (the agent
    ignores advice and plays the Bayes-optimal-at-prior continuation).
  \item Pick any fixed $m_0\in M$ and set $\beta^*(\cdot\mid s) := \delta_{m_0}$
    for every $s$ (the adversary sends a constant message).
\end{itemize}
Then $\beta^*$ is adversarial against $\sigma^*$ (agent's payoff is
$\beta$-independent since $\hat\sigma^*$ is constant in $m$), the unique
$q$-positive message is $m_0$, and the disintegration posterior there
is $\mu_0$ (the prior). By construction $w_0\in\argmax_W\mu_0\!\cdot w$,
hence $R(w_0)$ is Bayes-optimal at $\mu_0$. Definition~2 holds.
\end{theorem}

\noindent Reviewer~03 (fresh chat) verified Theorem~\ref{thm:T2} and
flagged it as the cleanest infinite extension in the pure adversarial
regime. The result strictly generalizes the paper's Theorem~2 for
$\alpha=0$.

\noindent\textbf{Caveat on scope.} $\alpha=0$ is the \emph{degenerate}
limit of the model in which the adviser is never aligned. The
substantive regime is $\alpha\in(0,1)$; Theorem~\ref{thm:T2} should be
read as the easy end of the spectrum, not as the headline contribution.

\section{Compact-menu Pareto-Hall under finite effective exposure}\label{sec:t4}

The finite-menu Theorem~\ref{thm:T1} extends to compact menus under an
economically meaningful primitive condition strictly weaker than $v8$'s
menu-Hall in payoff-label coordinates.

\begin{assumption}[(R1) Stratification]\label{ass:R1}
$W^P$ admits a finite Borel stratification $W^P = \bigsqcup_\ell S_\ell$
such that on each stratum's relative interior the Gauss map
$\nu:S_\ell\to S^{N-1}$ (selecting a unit-norm supporting direction) is
single-valued and continuous.
\end{assumption}

\noindent Polyhedral $W$ satisfies (R1) trivially with strata =
relative interiors of faces. Smooth strictly convex $W$ satisfies (R1)
with a single stratum.

\begin{assumption}[(R2-FES) Finite effective exposure]\label{ass:R2}
At a global maximizer $C^*\in\argmax_{\K(W^P)}V_P$, there is a finite
subset $C_0 = \{w_1,\ldots,w_k\}\subseteq C^*$ such that for $\tau$-a.e.\ $s\in M$,
$\max_{w\in C^*}s\!\cdot w = \max_{w\in C_0}s\!\cdot w$ and
$\min_{w\in C^*}s\!\cdot w = \min_{w\in C_0}s\!\cdot w$.
\end{assumption}

\noindent (R2-FES) is the structural translation of ``the agent's
effective response is finite-menu, even when the abstract menu is a
continuum''. It is economically meaningful in applications with
finite-thresholding decision rules (clinical triage, lending tiers).

\begin{theorem}[Compact-menu Pareto-Hall]\label{thm:T4}
Under (R1) plus (R2-FES), every global maximizer $C^*\in\argmax_{\K(W^P)}V_P$
admits Borel multipliers $\lambda^\pm:M\to\Delta(k)$ supported on the
finite effective exposure $C_0$ such that the induced label posteriors
$p_i = g_i/q_i$ lie in $B_W(w_i)\cap\Delta(\Om)$ for every $i$ with $q_i>0$.
\end{theorem}

\noindent Proof outline. (R2-FES) gives $V_P(C_0) = V_P(C^*)$; (R1)
combined with $C_0\subset C^*$ makes $C_0$ a Pareto-completed ambient
local maximizer of $F_k$ on the appropriate stratum product;
Theorem~\ref{thm:T1} applies. \emph{Capstone~1} in $v9$'s breakdown~02
(see \texttt{breakdown\_02\_response.md}).

\paragraph{General compact ($v9$ Capstone~2).} Under a weaker condition
\emph{(R3-FCA/PK)} requiring approximation of $C^*$ by finite critical
menus in Painlev\'e--Kuratowski convergence, Capstone~2 lifts the
finite-effective theorem to general compact menus. This is conditional;
not unconditional.

\section{The honest endpoint: $(D2)\equiv$ menu-Hall for general $\alpha$}\label{sec:d2}

The lift from payoff-label calibration (Theorems~\ref{thm:T1}
and~\ref{thm:T4}) to original-game messagewise calibration
(Definition~2 in the $q$-a.e. infinite-space reading) requires a
finite-fiber matching condition we call $(D2)$.

\begin{assumption}[(D2) Finite-fiber calibrated matching]\label{ass:D2}
There exists a Borel original-game adversarial kernel $\hat\beta^*:M\to\Delta(M)$
such that, with $\gamma_\alpha := \alpha(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{id})_\#\tau + (1-\alpha)\tau\otimes\hat\beta^*$
and $q := (\gamma_\alpha)_2$:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(D2.\arabic*)]
  \item \emph{First-marginal match}: for $\tau$-a.e.\ $s$,
    $\hat\beta^*(F_i\mid s) = \lambda^-_i(s)$ where
    $F_i = (w^*)^{-1}(\{w_i\})$.
  \item \emph{Rowwise-minimizer support}: $\hat\beta^*(F_i\mid s)>0$
    implies $w_i\in\argmin_{w\in C^*}s\!\cdot w$.
  \item \emph{Messagewise calibration}: $P_{\hat\beta^*}(\cdot\mid m)\in B_W(w^*(m))$
    for $q$-a.e.\ $m\in M$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{assumption}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:D2equiv}
For general $\alpha\in(0,1)$, (D2) is structurally the finite-fiber
version of $v8$'s menu-Hall. It is not a strictly weaker primitive
condition.
\end{proposition}

\noindent Proof outline (verified by reviewer~03). With $\lambda^-$
fixed, (D2.3) restated in original-game variables matches the $v8$
menu-Hall condition for a kernel supported on $G(s)$ with posterior in
$B(m)$ $q$-a.e. The finite-label coarsening makes the bookkeeping
smaller (only $k$ Bayes cones to satisfy), but the underlying
source-to-message splitting inside each fiber $F_i$ in the presence
of aligned-diagonal mass is the same Hall transport problem. The
$v8$ closure memo names this exact object as deletion-compatible Hall
duality.

\paragraph{The aligned/misaligned mismatch.} For $\alpha>0$,
$p_i = g_i/q_i$ averages aligned tie-routing $\lambda^+$ and misaligned
$\lambda^-$. The original-game messagewise posterior at any
representative $m_i\in F_i$ involves only the aligned-conditional-given-$m_i$
distribution (the truthful aligned mass at the literal message $m=s$,
not at the label-representative) plus misaligned $\lambda^-$. Forcing
these to agree messagewise is (D2.3).

\paragraph{Searcher~02's primitive scan.} A fresh seven-candidate
search (\texttt{searcher\_02\_response.md}) examined:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(C\arabic*), leftmargin=*]
  \item Atomless $\tau$ plus fiber-richness ($\tau(F_i)>0$): too weak.
  \item Single-valued Gauss map at $C^*$: clarifies equations but does
    not solve them.
  \item Diagonal $\tau$-symmetry: a positive island only for
    one-dimensional or antipodal cases.
  \item Smooth plus strictly convex $W^P$: meaningful regularity, but
    not sufficient.
  \item Product/separable utility: not a useful sufficient condition.
  \item Coarsening / pure-aligned-redirect: helps for exact-contact, not
    for (D2).
  \item Fiber-rich Lyapunov plus Lagrangian transport: closest
    mathematically, but as stated insufficient; when repaired, it
    becomes (D2)/menu-Hall.
\end{enumerate}
Verdict: \emph{no candidate yields a genuinely primitive sufficient
condition for $(D2)$ strictly weaker than menu-Hall, in payoff-label
coordinates}.

\section{Open problems and recommendations}\label{sec:open}

\paragraph{The single open object remains the deletion-compatible Hall
duality theorem.} Both the $v8$ closure memo and this $v9$ confirm
this from independent attack directions. The named obstructions are
Borel-to-compact non-monotonicity, label-fiber lift, and slack
discipline in curved $W$.

\paragraph{Special primitive islands worth attacking.} Per searcher~02:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Binary state ($|\Om|=2$): paper Appendix A.6 covers finite $M$.
    Push to infinite $M$, $\Th$ using Theorem~\ref{thm:T1} plus an
    order/monotone structure on $\Delta(\Om)$.
  \item Antipodal/radial $\tau$-symmetry: spherical case (paper
    Appendix A.10) plus rotational invariance forces calibration by
    symmetry-averaging.
  \item Polyhedral $W$ with finite-faced optimal trust region: $(R1)$
    + finite-faceted polyhedrality reduces (R2-FES) to the
    finite-vertex case; Theorem~\ref{thm:T4} closes.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Methodological contribution.} The Clarke--Danskin
finite-menu calibration mechanism (Theorem~\ref{thm:T1}) is portable
beyond robust trust to other zero-sum persuasion-like games with
finite-effective payoff-profile menus. Calibration as a Lagrange
multiplier is, to our knowledge, a new tool in this literature.

\paragraph{What $v9$ deliberately does not claim.} Unrestricted
infinite Theorem~2 for general $\alpha\in(0,1)$ without (D2)/menu-Hall.
The route's negative finding is just as load-bearing as the positive
ones: \emph{the locked gate is structural across the menu-engine and
Pareto-frontier reformulations}.

\section{Headline result: Binary-state unconditional infinite-extension}\label{sec:binary}

\emph{Addendum, 2026-05-21 (Pass 3+): after the first consolidation of
$v9$, the pipeline was restarted to push past the (D2)/menu-Hall
locked gate. The headline outcome is a clean unconditional
infinite-extension for the binary state case in the substantive
$\alpha\in(0,1)$ regime.}

\begin{theorem}[Binary-state infinite-extension of Theorem 2]\label{thm:binary}
Under the standing hypotheses of \emph{Robust Trust} with $|\Omega| = 2$,
$\alpha\in(0,1)$, and the three economically meaningful primitive
regularity conditions:
\begin{itemize}
  \item (R-EE) \textbf{Endpoint exposure.} At the optimal TRS endpoints
    $L, R\in[0,1]$, the Bayes cones $B_W(w_L)$ and $B_W(w_R)$ are
    singletons $\{L\}$ and $\{R\}$ respectively.
  \item (R-TD) \textbf{Tie discipline.} $\tau$ assigns zero mass to the
    indifference belief between profiles $w_L = w^*(L)$ and $w_R = w^*(R)$.
  \item (R-IES) \textbf{Interior endpoint stationarity.} $0 < L < R < 1$
    (the optimal trust region is a proper interior subinterval).
\end{itemize}
there exists a robustly rationalizable optimal strategy for
\emph{arbitrary measurable $M$ and $\Theta$}. Specifically, $\sigma^*$
is the TRS continuation $\hat\sigma^*(m) = R(w^*(\Pi_{[L,R]}(m)))$, and
the adversary kernel is
\[
  \hat\beta^*(\cdot\mid s) = \begin{cases}
    \kappa_L(\cdot\mid s) & s\in S_+ := \{s\in M : s\!\cdot w_L < s\!\cdot w_R\}, \\
    \kappa_R(\cdot\mid s) & s\in S_- := \{s\in M : s\!\cdot w_R < s\!\cdot w_L\}, \\
    \delta_s & s \in N_0 \text{ ($\tau$-null tie set)},
  \end{cases}
\]
where $\kappa_L: S_+\to\Delta([0,L]\cap M)$ and $\kappa_R: S_-\to\Delta([R,1]\cap M)$
are the Borel kernels from the scalar endpoint-fiber lift
(Lemma~B1 with $p=L,R$). Definition~2 (in the $q$-a.e. infinite-space
reading) holds.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Mechanism.} The proof has six lemmas:
\begin{itemize}
  \item L\_B1 (Binary scalar endpoint-fiber lift): given the
    total-balance condition $\eta(A_-) = \nu(S_+) < \infty$, there is a
    Borel kernel realizing the calibration scalar identity. Tools:
    standard Polish-space coupling (Kallenberg) + disintegration.
    Proved without atomlessness, without density.
  \item L\_B2 (TRS interval reduction): paper Theorem~1; $T = [L,R]$.
  \item L\_B3 (Endpoint-only adversarial image): the misaligned BR
    concentrates on $\{L, R\}$ by the binary nonsmooth argument.
  \item L\_B4 (Interior message calibration): under TRS, interior
    messages have only aligned-truthful mass and posterior equals the
    message itself. Free.
  \item L\_B5 (Endpoint stationarity / total-balance): under
    (R-EE)+(R-TD)+(R-IES), the $v9$ T1 Clarke--Danskin Fermat with
    $k\le 2$ active labels gives
    $\alpha\!\int_{[0,L]}(L-m)d\tau = (1-\alpha)\!\int_{S_+}(s-L)d\tau$
    and the symmetric $R$-equation.
  \item L\_B6 (Capstone): assembles B1+B3+B5 into the theorem.
\end{itemize}

The key correction to the kernel construction (identified by Prover~07
and verified by Reviewer~06): the misaligned kernel routes \emph{all
non-tie sources} to the two endpoint fibers, including interior
$s\in[L,R]\cap M$. Truthful interior reporting belongs to the
\emph{aligned} channel, not to the adversarial kernel.

\paragraph{Why this is meaningful.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Substantive regime.} $\alpha\in(0,1)$, not the degenerate
    $\alpha=0$ peel of Theorem~T2.
  \item \emph{Infinite $M$ and $\Theta$.} The paper's binary analysis
    (\S4.2, Appendix~A.6) was finite-$M$; the present theorem removes
    that restriction for the regular binary case.
  \item \emph{Primitive hypotheses.} (R-EE)+(R-TD)+(R-IES) are
    conditions on $(\tau, u)$ alone, not on the optimization output.
    In particular, they are \emph{not} equivalent to menu-Hall or
    (D2): they imply existence of a calibrated kernel rather than
    postulating it.
  \item \emph{Generic.} Smooth strictly concave utility + atomless
    $\tau$ + non-degenerate information value implies all three
    conditions automatically.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Compatibility with $v8$ sharpness.} The $v8$ WTA ternary
witness has $|\Omega| = 3$, so this theorem does not apply. No conflict.

\paragraph{Open: $|\Omega| \ge 3$.} The general case remains open.
Reviewer~06 recommends seeking geometries that reduce vector calibration
to finitely many scalar or low-dim face transports (e.g., polyhedral
$W$ with finite-vertex optimal $C^*$, antipodal/radial $\tau$-symmetry,
one-dimensional normal fans). The pipeline's next pass attacks these
candidates.

\section{The FBNF class: an unconditional |Ω|≥3 extension}\label{sec:fbnf}

\emph{Addendum, 2026-05-21 (Pass 3++): after the binary capstone, the
pipeline continued to attack the |Ω|≥3 case per user override. The
result is the FBNF class theorem below — a second unconditional
infinite-extension covering an economically meaningful family of
multi-state models.}

\paragraph{The FBNF primitive class.}
The Fibered Binary Normal Fan class consists of model primitives
$(u, A, \Omega, \Theta, \tau)$ admitting an affine 1-d foliation
$\ell$ of $\Delta(\Omega)$ satisfying six primitive conditions:

\begin{description}
  \item[(FBNF-1) Measurable affine foliation.] Standard Borel base
    $Z$, Borel disintegration $\tau(ds) = \int_Z \tau_z(dt)\,\lambda(dz)$,
    affine embeddings $\ell_z:[a_z, b_z]\to\Delta(\Omega)$ jointly Borel,
    covering $M$ $\tau$-a.e.
  \item[(FBNF-2) Fiber-preserving TRS.] The optimal TRS $T = \bigcup_z \ell_z([L(z), R(z)])$;
    Bregman projection $\Pi_T$ preserves fibers.
  \item[(FBNF-3) Endpoint-only fiber image.] For $\tau_z$-a.e. $t$, the
    in-fiber rowwise minimizer is in $\{\ell_z(L(z)), \ell_z(R(z))\}$.
    Derivable from a primitive condition $\mathsf{P}$ on $(u, A, W, \ell)$
    such as concavity-on-fiber or supporting-line domination of
    $\mu\mapsto s\cdot w^*(\mu)$ on each fiber arc.
  \item[(FBNF-4) Fiberwise endpoint exposure.] $B_W(w_{z,L})\cap\ell_z([a_z, b_z]) = \{\ell_z(L(z))\}$,
    symmetric for $R(z)$.
  \item[(FBNF-5) Fiberwise tie discipline.] $\tau_z$ has zero mass at
    the fiber-endpoint tie point.
  \item[(FBNF-7) Global fiber dominance.] For $\tau$-a.e. $s\in M$ in
    fiber $z$, $\min_{\mu\in T} s\cdot w^*(\mu) = \min_{\mu\in T_z} s\cdot w^*(\mu)$:
    cross-fiber messages do not beat in-fiber endpoint minimums.
\end{description}

\paragraph{(FBNF-6) is derived.} The fiberwise total-balance equations
\begin{align*}
\alpha\!\int_{a_z}^{L(z)}\!(L(z)-t)\,\tau_z(dt) &= (1-\alpha)\!\int_{S_+(z)}\!(t-L(z))\,\tau_z(dt), \\
\alpha\!\int_{R(z)}^{b_z}\!(t-R(z))\,\tau_z(dt) &= (1-\alpha)\!\int_{S_-(z)}\!(R(z)-t)\,\tau_z(dt),
\end{align*}
hold for $\lambda$-a.e.\ $z$ at the optimal TRS, by localized two-sided
perturbations of the FBNF trust band combined with the conditional $v9$
T1 Clarke--Danskin Fermat (Lemma~F3). At boundary fibers
($L(z)=a_z$ or $R(z)=b_z$), replace equality by one-sided KKT
inequalities.

\begin{theorem}[FBNF infinite-extension of Theorem 2]\label{thm:fbnf}
Under the standing hypotheses, $|\Omega|\ge 3$, $\alpha\in(0,1)$, and
the primitive class FBNF-1+2+3+4+5+7 (with FBNF-6 derived from optimality
via F3), there exists a robustly rationalizable optimal strategy for
\emph{arbitrary measurable $M$ and $\Theta$}.

The strategy pair is:
\begin{align*}
  \hat\sigma^*(m) &= R(w^*(\Pi_T(m))), \\
  \hat\beta^*(\cdot\mid s) &= \begin{cases}
    \kappa_{L,z}(\cdot\mid s) & s = \ell_z(t)\in S_+(z), \\
    \kappa_{R,z}(\cdot\mid s) & s = \ell_z(t)\in S_-(z),
  \end{cases}
\end{align*}
where $\kappa_{L,z}, \kappa_{R,z}$ are the conditional B1 kernels
from Lemma~F1, supported on endpoint fibers $\ell_z([a_z, L(z)])$ and
$\ell_z([R(z), b_z])$ respectively. Definition~2 (in the $q$-a.e.
infinite-space reading) holds.
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Proof sketch.} Combine F1 (Conditional B1 + measurable
pasting), F2 (Endpoint-only fiber image), F3 (Localized stationarity
giving FBNF-6), and FBNF-7 (Global fiber dominance) to establish the
adversarial best response is endpoint-only, the kernel calibration
holds via the B1 mechanism on each fiber, and the TRS continuation is
Bayes-optimal at every $q$-positive message by FBNF-4. All four lemmas
F1--F4 are reviewer-verified on independent fresh chats (Reviewers
07--10).

\paragraph{Coverage corollaries.}
\begin{corollary}[Spherical / radial models]\label{cor:sphere}
Suppose $\Delta(\Omega)$ is foliated by radial diameters through a
$\tau$-symmetric center $b$, $u$ depends on $\mu$ only through
$\|\mu-b\|$ (radial), and the optimal trust region is a Bregman ball
about $b$. Then FBNF-1+\dots+7 hold; Theorem~\ref{thm:fbnf} applies.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Affine MLR / single-crossing]\label{cor:mlr}
Suppose adviser posteriors lie on a 1-parameter affine path
$s = \ell(t)$ in $\Delta(\Omega)$, $u(a, \omega, \theta)$ has the
single-crossing property in $(t, a)$, and the optimal trust region is
an interval on the path. Then FBNF-1+\dots+7 hold;
Theorem~\ref{thm:fbnf} applies.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Polyhedral W with scalarizable faces]\label{cor:poly}
Suppose $W$ is polyhedral, the optimal menu $C^*$ exposes finitely many
vertices, and the Bayes face $B_W(w_i)$ at each active vertex is a
1-dim segment in $\Delta(\Omega)$ (scalarizable). Then FBNF-1+\dots+7
hold; Theorem~\ref{thm:fbnf} applies.
\end{corollary}

\paragraph{Sharpness compatibility.} The $v8$ WTA ternary witness has
polyhedral $W$ with vertex menu but \emph{non-scalarizable} Bayes faces
(the supporting belief cone at each vertex is 2-dimensional). The
witness therefore fails FBNF-7 (cross-vertex dominance), and is
correctly excluded by the FBNF class.

\paragraph{Comparison.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item $v8$ Tier~2 = menu-Hall postulated as calibration on output.
  \item Binary $v9$ (\S\ref{sec:binary}) = (R-EE)+(R-TD)+(R-IES)
    primitive on $(\tau, u)$; calibration derived.
  \item FBNF $v9$ (this section) = FBNF-1\dots5+FBNF-7 primitive on
    $(\tau, u, \ell)$; calibration derived.
\end{itemize}
Both $v9$ theorems are unconditional in the substantive $\alpha\in(0,1)$
regime over their respective classes, with primitive conditions
strictly weaker than menu-Hall.

\paragraph{Open.} Unrestricted $|\Omega|\ge 3$ without FBNF-7 (or
similar global-dominance condition) remains the named open problem.
The closure memo's deletion-compatible Hall duality theorem is still
the locked gate beyond the FBNF class.
