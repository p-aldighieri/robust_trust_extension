## Role

You are the Searcher. The binary case **|Ω| = 2** is now fully closed
in the substantive α ∈ (0,1) regime: the capstone theorem (Theorem
in prover_07_response.md, verified by reviewer_06_response.md)
gives a robustly rationalizable optimal strategy for arbitrary
measurable M, Θ under three economically meaningful primitive
conditions (R-EE), (R-TD), (R-IES), strictly weaker than menu-Hall.

Reviewer 06's next-step signal: **attack |Ω| ≥ 3 with geometries
that reduce vector calibration to finitely many scalar or
low-dimensional face transports**. The general deletion-compatible
Hall duality theorem otherwise remains the locked gate.

## Context — what made binary work

The binary success came from THREE features unique to |Ω| = 2:

1. **W^P is 1-dimensional curve** (in R²). The optimal menu C* is
   parametrizable by μ ∈ [L, R].
2. **Trust region T = [L, R] is an interval** with two endpoints,
   so the adversary's image is the 2-point set {L, R}.
3. **Bayes cones at endpoints are SINGLETONS**: B_W(w_L) = {L},
   B_W(w_R) = {R}. So calibration is a SCALAR matching condition.

For |Ω| ≥ 3, the analogues fail in general:
- W^P is (|Ω|-1)-dimensional manifold.
- T is a higher-dim convex compact subset of Δ(Ω); ∂T is (|Ω|-2)-dimensional.
- Bayes cones at non-vertex points are non-trivial (can be intervals,
  faces).

## Your job

Survey candidate geometries for |Ω| ≥ 3 where vector calibration
reduces to finitely many scalar/low-dim transports, mirroring the
binary structure:

### Candidate G1: Polyhedral W with finite-vertex optimal C*

If W is a polytope and the optimal C* consists of finitely many
vertices of W (extreme rays of W^P), then:
- C* = {w_1, ..., w_k} (finite-vertex menu).
- Active labels are finitely many.
- v9 T1 (finite-menu Pareto-Hall) applies directly in payoff-label
  coords.
- Bayes cones B_W(w_i) at vertices have specific polyhedral structure
  (faces of Δ(Ω)).

**Question**: under what primitive conditions on (u, A, Ω, Θ) does the
optimal C* consist of finitely many vertices of polyhedral W? Examples:
finite-action models, piecewise-linear utility, finite-experiment models.

**Calibration reduction**: each Bayes cone is a face of Δ(Ω), with
EXTREMAL points (corners of the face). The calibration at vertex w_i
needs the posterior in face B_W(w_i). Maybe each face is achievable
by a finite combination of source-side mass transports.

### Candidate G2: Antipodal / radial τ-symmetry

For state spaces |Ω| ≥ 3 with τ invariant under a finite group G
acting on Δ(Ω), AND the optimal C* G-orbit-saturated, calibration
may follow by symmetry-averaging.

**Question**: state precise stabilizer-rich symmetry conditions
(G acts on Δ(Ω) with continuous affine action; τ G-invariant;
u G-equivariant in (a, ω); optimal C* G-orbit-saturated). Under
these, does the conditional-source-given-label distribution
average along the orbit to give a calibrated posterior?

### Candidate G3: One-dim normal fan / fibered binary

If the Gauss map ν: W^P → S^{|Ω|-1} factors through a 1-dim
parameter (e.g., W^P is generated by a 1-parameter family of
supporting beliefs), then the trust region T inherits a fibered
structure where each fiber is a "binary" sub-problem.

**Question**: state the precise fibered structure. If T fibers
into binary problems (e.g., T is a curve in Δ(Ω) plus normal-
fan structure), can binary capstone be applied fiberwise?

### Candidate G4: Block-diagonal / product utility

If u(a, ω, θ) decomposes as block-diagonal across pairs of states:
u(a, ω, θ) = Σ_{(i,j)} u_{ij}(a, ω, θ) · 1_{ω ∈ {i,j}}, then the
problem may decompose into pairwise (binary) subproblems.

**Question**: how restrictive is this? Does it admit substantive
applications?

### Candidate G5: Triangulation reduction

W^P viewed as a triangulated complex (in |Ω|-dim). Each simplex
in the triangulation has finitely many vertices; on each simplex
binary or trinary local arguments may apply; calibration glues
across simplices.

**Question**: what triangulation conditions on W^P and τ make this
work?

### Candidate G6: NEW (you may add others)

Propose any other special primitive class for |Ω| ≥ 3 where
calibration reduces to finitely many low-dim transports.
