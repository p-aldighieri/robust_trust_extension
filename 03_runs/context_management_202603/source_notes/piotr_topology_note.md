# Piotr Note On General-Proof Difficulty

Recorded: 2026-03-10
Source: user report of direct conversation with Piotr Dworczak

## Note

Piotr indicated that the general proof attempts he tried did not work.

This does **not** imply that a general result is impossible. It does suggest that a successful extension beyond the finite-\(M\), finite-\(\Theta\) case is unlikely to come from a naive transplantation of the paper's finite argument.

The practical implication for the proof search is:

1. topology and compactness choices are likely central;
2. some additional restriction on the relevant spaces may be necessary;
3. any claimed unrestricted general proof should be treated with skepticism unless it explicitly resolves the topological/existence issues.

## Operational Use

Use this note as a negative prior against proof routes that:

1. reuse the finite minimax argument without new topological machinery;
2. assume existence of maximizers, saddle points, or regular conditional objects without checking the infinite-space hypotheses;
3. ignore the possibility that the right theorem may require added restrictions rather than a fully unrestricted extension.
