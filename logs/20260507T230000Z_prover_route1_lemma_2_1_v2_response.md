- **Variables:** $x_{B, u} \ge 0$ for $B \in \mathcal S$, $u \in U_{\mathcal P}$ — unscaled misaligned source mass from slab $B$ routed to column $u$.

**Constraints:**
- Row mass: $\sum_{u \in J(B)} x_{B, u} = t_B := \tau(B)$, where $J(B) := \{u : B \subseteq R_u^\eps\}$. $x_{B, u} = 0$ for $u \notin J(B)$.

The aligned mass $\alpha\,\tau(L_u)$ would then be zero for every $u$, and the LP loses its sourcewise aligned-attribution power.

- **Label-neighborhood regularization:** replace exact fibers $L_u$ with neighborhoods $L_u^\delta := \{s : \|w^*(s) - u\| < \delta\}$ for $\delta > 0$.
