# Reviewer pass — Route 2 Lemma 2.1 stall

You are the **Reviewer**. The Route 2 Lemma 2.1 prover stalled at three named obstructions. Audit the stall: are the obstructions structural (Route 2 R6 dead), or is one of the prover's three unblockers a clean primitive repair?

## Three named obstructions (verbatim from prover)

**(O1) Borel-to-compact deletion gap.** The natural Borel separation produces a primitive deletion functional $A_E(m) := \sum_i \lambda_i \mathbf{1}_{E_i}(m)(v_i - w^*(m))$ that passes the renaming test. But converting Borel $E_i$ to compact $K_i$ is not supplied by ordinary inner regularity: the inner term
$$\int_M\!\inf_{m \in G(s)}\,\sum_i \lambda_i \mathbf{1}_{E_i}(m)\,s\cdot(v_i - w^*(m))\,d\tau(s)$$
is non-monotone in $(E_i)$ because $s\cdot(v_i - w^*(m))$ is signed. Replacing $E_i$ with compact $K_i \subseteq E_i$ may remove the rowwise minimizing contact point and raise or lower the inf unpredictably.

**(O2) Continuum-mass / label-fiber lift gap.** Cell-flow LP variables $x_{rc}$ for mass moved from source cell $B_r$ to message cell $D_c$ cannot in general lift to Borel kernels supported on $R_0$. Two failure modes:
- If $x_{rc}$ is allowed whenever $(B_r \times D_c) \cap R_0 \ne \emptyset$, mass may flow from sources in $B_r$ that have no contact in $D_c$. The matrix is feasible in the LP but cannot be lifted.
- If $x_{rc}$ is allowed only when every $s \in B_r$ has a contact in $D_c$, the LP is too conservative and declares infeasibility where a measurable kernel exists.

**(O3) Slack discipline failure.** $n(\eps)\,\rho_\eps \to 0$ requires the compactification error to be uniform over all dual certificates generated by $V_\eps$. Standing hypotheses don't supply this modulus; the error could scale with vertex count or contact-relation complexity.

The prover's unblockers:
- **(U3)** A new deletion-compatible Hall duality theorem with uniform $o(1/n)$ compact-patch approximation.
- **(U-fiber)** Fiber-thickness or liftability hypothesis on $R_0$.
- **(U-Borel)** Replace $\Delta_\text{del}^{cp}$ with a Borel-patch residual (changes the route's renaming-test surface).
